2. What is StudyGuard?

StudyGuard is an Android application primarily designed for international students in Germany.

The app helps users organize areas such as studies, tasks, deadlines, exams, work planning, housing, budgeting, career planning, reminders and other student-life responsibilities.

3. Information stored inside the app

Depending on how you use StudyGuard, you may enter information such as:

  • tasks and personal notes,
  • deadlines and dates,
  • module and exam information,
  • study planning information,
  • work-planning information,
  • housing checklist information,
  • budget and financial planning values,
  • career planning information,
  • app preferences,
  • reminder settings.

This information is stored locally on your Android device for the purpose of providing StudyGuard's functionality.

StudyGuard does not operate a developer cloud backend that receives this app content.

4. Accounts

StudyGuard does not require a user account or login.

Therefore, StudyGuard does not maintain a remote StudyGuard user profile or account database.

5. Advertising

The Google Play version of StudyGuard does not contain advertising or AdMob.

6. Analytics and tracking

The Google Play version of StudyGuard does not use analytics SDKs or developer-operated behavioural tracking.

StudyGuard does not use your locally stored app content to create advertising profiles.

7. Selling of data

StudyGuard does not sell your locally stored app content.

8. Android system functions

StudyGuard may use Android system functionality to provide features such as:

  • local notifications and reminders,
  • home-screen widgets,
  • opening a calendar entry draft,
  • opening another application when you explicitly request it.

These actions may involve Android or another application installed on your device.

External applications and Android system services may process information independently under their own privacy policies.

9. Support communication

If you contact StudyGuard support by email, we receive the information you voluntarily provide.

This may include your email address, your message, screenshots or other attachments you choose to send.

This information is used only to respond to your request, provide support, investigate reported problems or handle necessary follow-up communication.

Support email communication is separate from the information stored locally inside the StudyGuard app.

10. Data retention

Information stored locally in StudyGuard remains on your device until you:

  • delete it inside StudyGuard,
  • clear StudyGuard's application storage through Android, or
  • uninstall the application.

Because StudyGuard does not provide a developer-operated cloud backup or user account for this content, the developer generally cannot remotely access, recover or delete information that exists only on your device.

11. Support email retention

Support communications are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, investigate technical issues and comply with applicable legal or security obligations.

12. Data sharing

StudyGuard does not share locally stored StudyGuard content with advertisers, data brokers or analytics providers.

Information may leave StudyGuard when you deliberately use a feature that opens another app or external service, or when you voluntarily send information to StudyGuard support.

13. Security

StudyGuard follows a local-first approach intended to reduce unnecessary transmission of personal information.

The security of locally stored information also depends on your Android device, operating-system security, screen lock, device backups and other security settings.

Users should keep their Android device updated and appropriately protected.

14. Privacy-policy website

This privacy policy is hosted using GitHub Pages.

StudyGuard does not add advertising or its own analytics tracking to this privacy-policy page.

GitHub may process technical information necessary to deliver this website according to GitHub's own privacy practices.

GitHub Privacy Statement: GitHub General Privacy Statement

15. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when StudyGuard's features, data practices or legal requirements change.

If a future StudyGuard version introduces additional data collection, cloud services, analytics, advertising, accounts or other relevant functionality, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated accordingly.

2. Was ist StudyGuard?

StudyGuard ist eine Android-App, die sich insbesondere an internationale Studierende in Deutschland richtet.

Die App unterstützt bei der Organisation von Studium, Aufgaben, Fristen, Prüfungen, Arbeitsplanung, Wohnung, Budgetplanung, Karriereplanung, Erinnerungen und weiteren Bereichen des studentischen Alltags.

3. In der App gespeicherte Informationen

Je nach Nutzung kannst du beispielsweise folgende Informationen in StudyGuard eintragen:

  • Aufgaben und persönliche Notizen,
  • Fristen und Termine,
  • Modul- und Prüfungsinformationen,
  • Studienplanung,
  • Arbeitsplanung,
  • Wohnungs-Checklisten,
  • Budget- und Finanzplanungswerte,
  • Karriereplanung,
  • App-Einstellungen,
  • Erinnerungseinstellungen.

Diese Informationen werden zur Bereitstellung der StudyGuard-Funktionen lokal auf deinem Android-Gerät gespeichert.

StudyGuard betreibt kein Entwickler-Cloud-Backend, an das diese App-Inhalte übertragen werden.

4. Benutzerkonten

StudyGuard benötigt kein Benutzerkonto und keinen Login.

7. Verkauf von Daten
